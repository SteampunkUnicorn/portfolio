    02 / BEHIND THE BUILD

    Playful on the surface.
    Purposeful underneath.

    01 / THE MODEL

    Ingredients as data.

    Steps and choices are generated from JSON, keeping content separate from the component rendering and selection rules.

    02 / THE STATE

    Constraints that make sense.

    Single-choice steps replace existing selections, proteins have a limit, and selected ingredients stay in sync with their visual representation.

    03 / THE INTERACTION

    Multiple ways to build.

    Desktop users can drag or click ingredients; smaller screens get a select control. The finished composition updates layer by layer.
