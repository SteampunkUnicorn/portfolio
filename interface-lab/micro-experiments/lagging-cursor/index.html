Cursor Labels

A ring trails the pointer with easing and swells into a labeled badge over marked elements.

Button One
Video One
Under the hood

What’s happening?

The cursor ring follows the pointer with a bit of easing, creating a sense of momentum instead of snapping directly to each movement. When the pointer enters an element marked with a data-cursor attribute, the ring expands and displays its label.

The effect separates pointer tracking from interaction context: the ring handles movement, while each target supplies its own label through HTML. That makes the behavior reusable across buttons, media, and other interactive elements without hardcoding a different animation for each one.
