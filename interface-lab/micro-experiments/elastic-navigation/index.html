Small experiment / 005

Elastic Navigation.

An active navigation indicator stretches between destinations.

Explore is selected.

Controls

Tab between buttons and press Enter or Space. The indicator follows the active state; keyboard focus stays visible.

Under the hood

What’s happening?

A single absolute-positioned indicator animates between the measured tab positions. The selected state uses aria-current; every item remains a standard button, and the focus ring is independent from the decorative animation. The indicator snaps immediately when reduced motion is preferred.
