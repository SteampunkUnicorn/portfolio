What’s happening?

A small particle simulation uses velocity, damping, pointer repulsion, and wall collisions. Drawing happens on Canvas; semantic buttons provide alternative interactions. The animation can pause, is paused initially for reduced-motion preferences, and does not trap pointer or keyboard focus.
