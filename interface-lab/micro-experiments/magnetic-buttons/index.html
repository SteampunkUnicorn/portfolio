Magnetic Buttons.

A button that reacts to your pointer without compromising its hit target.

Keep the pointer nearby. Try Tab and Enter, too.

Controls

Under the hood

What’s happening?

The button moves using a translated visual layer, rather than changing its actual focus or click target. Keyboard focus stays stable; pointer motion can be disabled and respects reduced-motion preferences.
