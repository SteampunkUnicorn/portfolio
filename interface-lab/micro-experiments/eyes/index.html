Small experiment / 011

Boo!

Each pupil points at the cursor from anywhere on the page and stays inside its eye.

Under the hood

What’s happening?

The ghost’s eyes track the pointer independently, with each pupil calculating its direction and distance relative to the center of its own eye. `Math.atan2()` determines the angle toward the pointer, while `Math.hypot()` measures the distance and a clamp limits how far the pupil can travel.

CSS handles the appearance and movement: the white eye provides a viewport for the pupil, `overflow: hidden` keeps it contained, and a short transform transition smooths its movement. The ghost itself floats separately using CSS keyframes, combining ambient animation with pointer-driven interaction.

The result is a character that feels responsive without needing a full animation library. The ghost’s body, floating motion, and eye tracking each have a distinct responsibility, making the effect relatively simple to maintain and customize.
