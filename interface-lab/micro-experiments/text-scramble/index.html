The headline machine

Controls

The scrambled characters are decorative; assistive technology receives the final phrase as one stable announcement.

Under the hood

What’s happening?

A time-based loop progressively resolves random characters into the final string. The effect is presentation only: the result is announced once via a separate polite status region. Reduced motion skips intermediate frames, and autoplay is opt-in rather than automatic.
