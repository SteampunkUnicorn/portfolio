What’s happening?

The swatches are specified with oklch(), which expresses hue, chroma, and perceptual lightness separately. The demonstration calculates WCAG 2 contrast from computed sRGB values where available. Out-of-gamut rendering and forced-colors modes need browser verification; a contrast ratio does not establish full accessibility by itself.
