          05 / THE IMPORTANT BIT

          Accessibility isn't a filter.

          These examples intentionally isolate a few common problems. They are teaching demos, not automated WCAG audits or accurate simulations of particular disabilities or assistive technologies.

          Test actual projects with keyboard-only navigation, browser zoom, screen readers, and the accessibility tree. Check contrast and motion preferences as part of the broader testing process.
